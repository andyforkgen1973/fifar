FIFAR Golf Day – Friday 1st April 2022

Dear all,

The next FIFAR golf day has been booked for Friday 1st April at Aylesbury Vale Golf Club, near Stewkley. The cost is £45 per person, which includes 18 holes of golf, bacon rolls on arrival and lunch of ham, egg and chips. This cost is applicable for all attendees, even if you are a member of the club.
