FIFAR Golf Day June 2023 Results

The 2023 FIFAR Golf Day took place at Aylesbury Vale Golf Club on the 2nd of June, and we were blessed with the weather. Yet again we had great support on the day with over 30 players competing for Individual, Team, Longest Drive and Nearest the Pin competitions. After being served a well-deserved ham, egg and chips and once the awards had been presented, we were able to auction a few prizes along with the collection of the traditional FIFAR fines – such as forgetting your putter!

I’m pleased to confirm that the money raised from the golf, auction and fines has reached an amazing amount of £850.00.

Just want to take this opportunity to thank you all for your continued support. The money raised will certainly go towards supporting football within our local area.

See you all at the next event.

Winners

  • Nearest the pinMartin Gillett
  • Longest driveAlex Price
  • Individual winner (40 pts)Bruce Culbert
  • Team winnersNeil Thompson, Pete Rackshaw, Darren Saunders & Andy Pepin
