Golf Day 2024 Results

Following another successful day at Aylesbury Vale Golf Club we had over 30 playing golfers who have yet again contributed to the FIFAR fundraising cause. We were blessed with some good weather, which has certainly been a rarity this year.

Just want to say a big thank you to all that were involved and congratulate everyone in raising over £900.00 through the auction and, of course, what is now becoming the infamous FIFAR fines.

The winners

  • Overall winnerJack Milsom
  • Team winnersRussell McCafferty, Lee Phillips, Mark Brewster
  • Nearest the pin – Hole 8Mark Ledlie
  • Nearest the pin – Hole 17 (much harder hole 😉)Andy Quigley
