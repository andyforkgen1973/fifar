The Ken Sheppard Memorial RRFC Veterans Reunion Match

“The Ken Sheppard Memorial RRFC Veterans Reunion Match”
Also a fundraiser for RRFC’s brand new ‘Family Stand’, due March 2022.
@ The BEP Stadium – Sunday 31st October 2021, 11.30am KO

Through the recent season successes and climb up the divisions achieved by our RRFC First Team, we are now embarking on our next major development at the club: raising the funds to build a new 100-seater far side stand to increase our capacity and safety for our supporters and welcome more families to our club.

We are combining a fundraising reunion match event with a celebration in memory of a long-standing Committee Member who we sadly lost last year, Ken Sheppard, who dedicated his life to Risborough Rangers FC. We are hosting over 50 ex-RRFC players in a mini round-robin 11-a-side tournament and welcome all supporters to join us for some great football, memories, lots of legendary players and to help raise money for our latest venture.

Ex-RRFC players: if you are interested in playing in the tournament, please get in touch and we can let you know the format and details of the matches.

Supporters: entry will be free, but all donations welcome. Please spread the word and encourage friends and family to join us. We look forward to seeing you!
