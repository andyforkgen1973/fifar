FIFAR Golf Day 1st April 2022 Results

Another very successful golf day was held at Aylesbury Vale Golf Club where we had 35 playing golf on a day where the weather was kind, with only a couple of snow showers! During the presentation, and in true FIFAR golf society spirit, each team gave out fines to each other and, along with the auctioning of 4-ball vouchers, we were able to boost the overall funds by £470.00.

Thank you so much to all that took part.

  • Overall winner (40 pts)Chris Rymer
  • 2nd (39 pts)David Williams
  • 3rd (37 pts)George Magnocavallo
  • Longest driveAndy Quigley
  • Nearest the pinNo winner – the committee fined everyone for missing the green (apart from FIFAR 👍)

The Kevin Smees Memorial Cup

A team event with the best Stableford score 3 of 4, won by Mark Ledlie, Rino Palmigiano, George Magnocavallo and Frank Magnocavallo.
