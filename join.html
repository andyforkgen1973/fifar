Join FIFAR

Here’s how to join FIFAR – two simple steps and you’re on the team sheet.

Joining FIFAR is temporarily suspended whilst we fix some glitches. Check back soon.
01

Create your subscription

Click the Subscribe button below to start a yearly subscription of £10. PayPal will open and your payment will be taken securely – you need to set up a PayPal account or log in with an existing one to set up this subscription. You can cancel the subscription at any time via PayPal. Once you have paid, come back to this page and complete step 2.

Subscribe with PayPal – £10/year
02

Send us your details

Fill out the form below with your details and click Send. We will then process your membership and add you to our members list within 72 hours. If you are not added within 72 hours please contact us.

This opens your email app with your details filled in, ready to send to the FIFAR committee.
