Welcome to FIFAR

Risborough Rangers is proud to announce FIFAR (Friends in Football at Risborough). For over 45 years Risborough Rangers has grown into a club that provides football for all ages and abilities, including girls’ football and Pan-disability teams.

In order to provide the opportunities and experiences that the club aspires to give all its players, it has been decided to create a funding arm of the club – hence FIFAR has been formed.

FIFAR not only has the purpose of reuniting players, managers, coaches, volunteers, parents and all those that have been supporting the club throughout its history but, equally as important, reuniting Friends! We want everyone who is a friend of Risborough, our schools and football to join this newly formed charity. An annual fee of £10 is your joining fee; this annual subscription gives members open access to organised events such as nights out, golf tournaments and other social events which help this cause.

So if you are a Friend of Risborough, an existing or past player, manager, coach, volunteer or parent, or have been supporting the club in any way through its 45-year history, then follow the steps below:

  1. 1
    Pay your £10 subscriptionSecure yearly subscription via PayPal.
  2. 2
    Send us your detailsWe’ll add you to the squad list within 72 hours.

Start the process

There must be hundreds of players that have played over the years, along with managers, coaches and people that have helped along the way – so please register your details and join FIFAR today to re-unite with old associates, friends and colleagues!
